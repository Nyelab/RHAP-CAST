RHAPCAST

RIVER HERRING AVOIDANCE PROGRAM FORECAST

The River Herring Avoidance Program Forecast (RHAPCAST) forecasts regions to maximize chances of encountering target species (Atlantic herring or Atlantic mackerel) while minimizing chances of encountering river herring bycatch. Forecasts are provided about one week in advance to help you plan. Look for a new forecast each Monday! For all the maps below, warm colors like red and brown show places where the species is unlikely to be found. Cool colors like blue show places where species are more likely to be found.

December 20 – 26 Forecast to maximize Atlantic herring encounters while minimizing river herring encounters

The blue areas on the map show places where Atlantic herring are likely to be found. The red and brown areas show places where river herring are predicted, or where Atlantic herring are not predicted. We recommend going to the blue areas to maximize chances of encountering Atlantic herring and minimize risk of encountering river herring bycatch. To estimate this for each location, we combined the two species’ encounter probabilities.
