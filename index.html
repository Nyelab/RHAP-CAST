RHAPCAST

RIVER HERRING AVOIDANCE PROGRAM FORECAST

The River Herring Avoidance Program Forecast (RHAPCAST) maps identify areas to encounter target species (Atlantic herring or Atlantic mackerel) and avoid encountering river herring bycatch. Forecasts are provided about one week in advance to help you plan. Look for a new forecast each Monday! The model uses historical presence-absence data from NMFS trawl surveys, historical environmnetal data, and forecasted sea surface temperature data to predict where we expect the relevant species to be distributed a particular week.

July 18, 2026 to July 24, 2026 forecast to catch Atlantic herring and avoid river herring

The blue areas on the map (Avoid river herring and catch Atlantic herring map) show places where Atlantic herring are likely to be present and where river herring are likely to be absent. The red and brown areas show places where river herring are likely to be present or where Atlantic herring are likely to be absent. We recommend going to the blue areas. Click on the map for more information on the exact encounter probability, and spatial coordinates can be found at the bottom left of the map. A grid cell is approximately 130 km2 (height ~ 9 km and width ~ 14 km). Please refer to state and federal regulations for any restrictions.
