RIVER HERRING AVOIDANCE PROGRAM FORECAST

The River Herring Avoidance Program Forecast (RHAPCAST) forecasts regions to maximize encounters of target species (Atlantic herring or Atlantic mackerel) while minimizing river herring bycatch. Forecasts are provided about one week in advance to help you plan. Look for a new forecast each Monday! For all the maps below, warm colors like red and brown show places where the species is unlikely to be found. Cool colors like blue show places where it’s more likely to be found.

December 13 – 19 Forecast to maximize Atlantic herring encounters while minimizing river herring encounters

The blue areas on the map show places where Atlantic herring are likely to be found on their own. The red and brown areas show places where river herring are also likely to show up, or where Atlantic herring aren’t very common. We recommend going to the blue areas to maximize catch of your target species and minimize river herring bycatch. To estimate this for each location, we combined the two species’ encounter probabilities.
